🔥 How to actually lose belly & chest fat

You can't burn fat from one spot by training it — fat leaves your whole body when you eat slightly less than you burn. Do this:

• Eat ~300–400 kcal under maintenance.
• Protein ~110 g/day (chicken, eggs, fish, dairy, legumes).
• Keep the cardio finishers.
• Lift heavy (this app) to build the shape underneath.
• 7–8 h sleep, 8k+ steps/day.
